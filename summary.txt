World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026: U.S.-mediated Russia-Ukraine-U.S. talks ended a second day in Abu Dhabi after nearly two hours with no breakthrough on ending the war (AP, Reuters). Envoys agreed in principle on a large prisoner exchange; U.S. envoy Jared Kushner called it the biggest planned humanitarian release, with figures still to be set, and said he and Steve Witkoff would meet Vladimir Putin in Moscow on Thursday, then Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Dublin. Russia's Vladimir Medinsky said the sides found common ground on some issues but not others and dismissed a European role for now (Anadolu, AP, RFE/RL).

At COP31 in Antalya, Turkey, UN Secretary-General António Guterres told the opening leaders' summit that even fully implemented national plans pointed to more than 2.5°C of warming, called it "make or bake time," and urged a climate justice compact including taxes on fossil-fuel profits (UN News). President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan said decades of talks had not matched ideas with implementation; Brazil's Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva warned that wars and trade disputes were crowding out climate action. The U.S. stayed away for a second year (Reuters).

Iran's army said an oil tanker caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz after hitting a mine in a restricted area; the UK Maritime Trade Operations centre reported a tanker incident, with crew and damage unknown. Reuters could not independently verify the reports (AP, Reuters).

Spain held its first general strike in 14 years over housing costs, backed by unions UGT and CCOO (Reuters, The Guardian). In Argentina, Pope Leo XIV celebrated a closing Mass at Our Lady of Luján before continuing to Peru (Vatican, Reuters). Israeli airstrikes killed three Palestinians in Gaza, medics said, including two brothers at a tent for displaced people in Gaza City's Sheikh Radwan neighbourhood (Reuters). Iran designated Argentina's navy and air force as terrorist organizations after Buenos Aires listed the IRGC in April (Reuters).
